# ennUI

**A minimal, text-based home screen for KOReader.**
ennUI replaces KOReader's file browser with a quiet, distraction-free landing screen: just the information and shortcuts you actually want, arranged the way you want them, in a typeface you chose.

## Why ennUI

- **Built for e-ink.** No animations, no unnecessary redraws. The screen only updates when you touch it.
- **Genuinely minimal.** Text only, no icons or cover art cluttering the screen. What you see is what you configured, nothing more.
- **Deeply customizable without being complicated.** Every element has its own settings, but the defaults are sane enough that you never have to touch them if you don't want to.
- **Fast.** Closing a book takes you straight back to your home screen, not a generic file list.

## Features

**A home screen you design**
- Choose which sections appear (Date, Clock, Weather, and your Plugin entries) and reorder them to taste.
- Each section gets its own typeface, size, letter spacing, color, alignment, padding, and text effect (a drop shadow or a full outline).
- Pick a text color and Build Mode together, or go entirely your own way: ennUI doesn't assume a "default" look matters more than yours.

**A launcher for everything else**
- Add any installed KOReader plugin as a one-tap entry on your home screen.
- Organize entries into folders, one level deep, for as much or as little structure as you like.
- Reorder, rename, and manage entries right from the device, with no config files to hand-edit.
- A dedicated "Resume current book" entry gets you back into your last read in one tap.

**Weather, on your terms**
- A simple current-conditions line, fetched by ZIP code.
- Refreshes once per KOReader session and whenever you tap it, never on a background timer, never without you asking.

**Make it yours, then save it**
- A wallpaper, if you want one: fit, fill, stretch, or original size, with adjustable lightening so your text stays readable.
- Save your whole look as a named preset and switch between them instantly. Keep a "reading at night" preset and a "daytime" preset, or just experiment freely, knowing you can always get back to where you started.

**Stays out of your way**
- Settings live one swipe away, not on the home screen itself.
- Long-press any section (with Build Mode on) to jump straight to its settings, no hunting through menus.
- Everything is readable and legible first: careful defaults, sensible fallbacks, and clear feedback when something needs your attention.

## Who it's for

If you've ever wished KOReader's file browser was less of a file browser, and want your e-reader's home screen to feel considered rather than incidental, ennUI is built for you.

## Installation

Copy the `ennui.koplugin` folder into KOReader's `plugins` folder and restart KOReader. Full setup and configuration details are in [the plugin's own README](./ennui.koplugin/README.md).
